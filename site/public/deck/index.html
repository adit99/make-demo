THE CORE WEDGE

Structured UX people can use.
Agents that work around it.

Tables, forms, calendars, and fields give people a stable place to see, decide, and act.

The app is the shared state.

Agents and communication channels operate on the same records and permissions. Changes that matter stay visible and reviewable.

Product direction: autonomous actions, chat conversations, live email integration, and SMS are proposed capabilities. Today, the prototype supports imported context and AI app creation/editing.

THE TECHNICAL BET

Every new app inherits
the place it lives.

WHAT PEOPLE SHAPE

The interface, fields, views, and useful actions.

WHAT MAKE PROVIDES

Persistent records, identity, sharing, permissions, and context.

WHAT THE MODEL DOES

Compose the interface and interpret context. Everyday controls run as ordinary software.

A new household tool is another app in Make, with no separate hosting or distribution setup for its creator.

Implemented locally with React, OpenUI, a Node API, SQLite, and app-scoped invitations. Public hosting of the working backend and production identity are still ahead. OpenUI architecture ↗

START WITH HOUSEHOLDS

A small proving ground.
A much larger possibility.

Families have recurring needs, shared context, and tools that need to change.

01Will people come back and use what they make?

02Will another person join and contribute?

03Can the app evolve without losing their work?

The hypothesis: useful personal software can accumulate inside one trusted environment, serving households and eventually other small groups.
